    How it works

    Three steps, no setup. Built for bench scientists who want a sanity check before spending a week on sample prep.

    01 INGEST

    Paste a sequence

    Drop in a single protein in plain text or FASTA. We clean and validate it client-side before sending anything.

    02 ANALYZE

    Score it against known native MS data

    The model checks length, charge profile, hydrophobic patches, disorder, and other features known to predict whether a protein survives native ionization.

    03 DELIVER

    Get a score and clear next steps

    You see a suitability score from 0 to 100, the specific risk factors that hurt it, and concrete buffer and instrument recommendations.

    About

    Built solo as a weekend project. Trained on public datasets from the PDB and UniProt. Honest about limitations: this is a v1, validate critical experiments yourself.

    What this is. A first-pass screening tool. Useful for ranking candidates before committing instrument time. Not a substitute for empirical optimization, and not a guarantee of success.
    Known limitations. Trained on ~133 proteins (69 native MS validated + 64 proxy negatives from Swiss-Prot). The model under-predicts for sequences shorter than 50 amino acids and is less reliable for very large complexes (>1000 aa). Cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.87 on the training distribution; generalization to unusual sequences is uncertain. Treat scores as guidance, not as definitive predictions.
